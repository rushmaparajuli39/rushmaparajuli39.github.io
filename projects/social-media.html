What I do

  1. PlanA content calendar for each brand, built around what its audience responds to.
  2. PublishPhotos, short-form video (Reels), and link posts, adapted for each platform.
  3. SchedulePosts queued in Meta Business Suite's planner, twice a day, weeks ahead.
  4. MeasureViews, reach beyond followers, and watch time in Insights, then adjust the mix.

Results

Most views come from people who don't follow the Page yet, so the content is reaching new audiences, not only existing customers.

What the numbers told me

  • Podcast: short video carries it. Reels brought 84% of views, and the best post reached 1,906 views, so short clips are the priority.
  • Restaurant: food photos do the work, with 63% of views. It was also the biggest audience: 53,028 views and over 20 hours of watch time in 90 days.
  • Beauty studio (Threading & Waxing Experts): link posts and Reels split the views almost evenly, so both stay in the mix.
